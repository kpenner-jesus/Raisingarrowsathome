How much you'll receive

Based on your child's age.

Ages 5–8 $375up to $500 spent
Ages 8–12 $500up to $667 spent
Ages 12–15 $650up to $867 spent
Ages 15–18 $750up to $1000 spent

Common questions

How long until I hear back?

Within 30 days of submitting your written questionnaire and short video interview.

What's eligible for reimbursement?

Curriculum, workbooks, and educational books only. We don't cover field trips, school supplies, or extracurricular fees.

How does payment work?

Two payouts per month — on the 15th and the last day — via e-transfer from CEO Ministries.

Who qualifies?

Christian families considering homeschool for the first time who have never previously registered with the government.
